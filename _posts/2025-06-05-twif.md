### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 05 Jun 2025, Week 23

#### F-Droid core

Latest Client has been out for the last 2 weeks. _How does it feel? Modernity at its highest? A good start? Not expressive enough? Still rocking?_ We are open to all comments and critiques, specially those that come with a stack-trace.

_Not have it yet? Still looking like it's 2016?_ We can help! In F-Droid Client or Basic, on its own page, either scroll down, expand Versions and install the latest, or in the upper right menu choose “Allow Beta updates”. We are waiting for more feedback to deem this version stable and push it to everyone.

We haven't talked about the website lately, it's low profile as usual, we've fixed some bugs and there's work in progress to modernize its infrastructure. What can be seen are the new translations, so if your native language is [Basque](https://f-droid.org/2025/06/01/basque-language.html) or [Catalan](https://f-droid.org/2025/06/01/catalan-language.html), or you just like them, you can now select them in the language selector and enjoy the text.

[@shuvashish76](https://forum.f-droid.org/u/shuvashish76) makes a time leap:

Back in [April 17](https://f-droid.org/2025/04/17/twif.html#community-news) we mentioned the _“Open Letter to Organic Maps Shareholders”_ and there were some updates to this story. As time passed and things didn't move fast enough for the letter authors, the community thought more about forking, hence [CoMaps was born](https://news.itsfoss.com/organic-maps-fork-comaps/). They've already setup [governance](https://codeberg.org/comaps/Governance) and [FAQ](https://codeberg.org/comaps/Governance/src/branch/main/FAQ.md) docs to establish how the project will develop and we are working with the team for inclusion in F-Droid as you read this.
