### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 03 Jul 2025, Week 27

#### F-Droid core

Unattended updates spoiled us all, updates come and go, no need to ever open the app unless we want to scratch a new app itch. Between the dozen mirrors, delta index updates and our familiar palette, F-Droid Client got little complaints on the new theme, we guess that's a good thing.

Where we got complaints were about Android 10 and older, when using the bottom buttons navigation bar, as that one will cover the "Versions" toggle. A fix is already getting positive answers, until that's out there, affected users can rotate the screen and still interact.

Categories already grew with 20, did you scroll them a bit? Are there too many or we need more? Feed-back welcomed.

#### Community News

**[Canta](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.samo_lego.canta)** was updated to `2.5.1` but the appid was changed. So this week we introduce the new **[Canta](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.samolego.canta)**, _"Uninstall any\(\*\) app without root\!_, yes, same app name too, so if you did not install the app this week, you might want to uninstall the old one and... Ok, I see it now, same app name, same app icon? Umm, we can open each one, the old one has a "Warning" on top of the description, let's skip, we shall install the other one, no warning and version `3.0.0`.

**[Compose Stopwatch](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.justdeax.composeStopwatch)** was updated to `1.8.3` and [had a key change](https://justdeax.github.io/ComposeStopwatch/key-rotation), if you did not install the app this week, you might want to uninstall the old one and reinstall.

**[DAVx⁵](https://f-droid.org/packages/at.bitfire.davdroid)** was updated to `4.5-ose` and brings a nice feature: [UnifiedPush](https://f-droid.org/2022/12/18/unifiedpush.html) support. Read the [changelog](https://github.com/bitfireAT/davx5-ose/releases/tag/v4.5-ose) to get info and tutorials on how to enable this great feature for your app.

**[Fennec F\-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.mozilla.fennec_fdroid)** was updated to `140.0.0` with a short [changelog](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/android/140.0/releasenotes/) and many security fixes. We also got wind of something that is prepared for the next release, [Fennec will support UnifiedPush](https://fosstodon.org/@unifiedpush/114772493526546328).

But it's _not all roses_, as version `140.0.0` broke search engines selection, basically monopoly engines were re-added and made the default, overriding the user chosen one and even deleting custom ones. We are tracking this issue in [#120](https://gitlab.com/relan/fennecbuild/-/issues/120), and in the meantime, do check your Fennec settings and fix them as needed.

**[OsmAnd~](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.osmand.plus)** was updated to `5.1.2`, not suggested yet as usual, so update manually. The improvements list is [extensive](https://osmand.net/blog/osmand-android-5-1-released/) and this version marks ["15 years of OSMAnd"](https://osmand.net/blog/stats-15/). Hands down the most used feature of a map is its navigation, and the team is working on some [impressive algorithms](https://osmand.net/blog/fast-routing).

**[Status: Ethereum Crypto Wallet](https://f-droid.org/packages/im.status.ethereum)** was updated to `2.34.0` and these changes might need your attention specially if your current version is `2.31` or older.
Read why in the [team post](https://status.app/blog/status-v2-34-more-new-chains-market-centre-real-time-token-list-more).
