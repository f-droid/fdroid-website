### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 14 Aug 2025, Week 33

#### F-Droid core

After the last TWIF we pushed the latest version of **F-Droid Client** and **Basic** as suggested, so if the _About_ screen does not say `1.23.1` you need to update now.

We've been continuing to look for fixes in the updates department and we focused on the issue brought up by [AGP 8.12.0](https://f-droid.org/2025/08/07/twif.html#f-droid-core) last week. Apps that were not reproducible build are fixed in our build recipes for now, and for those that are reproducible build, we've worked with the app developers to temporarily downgrade AGP.

An issue was raised with Google [here](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/438515318), but we're not only waiting for that and will work to upgrade the "ware" as needed, as it will improve our processing speed as well. While _"don't fix it if it ain't broke"_ helped us focus in other areas that needed our attention, and we have plenty of those, we've reached the limits of what software cycle optimizations can gain us. Our weekly 100-200 apps built fit the human and hardware sweet spot, yet any of the 4 or 5 heavy apps can push other smaller ones to skip a cycle, worse, if they happen to update at the same time and we're not even talking about other "surprise" type problems like we had last month. 

#### Community News

Unrelated to our struggles above, Dries Buytaert has a nice 20 minutes long post about ["Funding Open Source like public infrastructure"](https://dri.es/funding-open-source-like-public-infrastructure) which aligns with our goals and aspirations. Most users know F-Droid as the ~~store~~app catalog, but when you install an app it's because the web server, the storage server, the signing server, the build server, the mirrors, the signing code, the build code, the scanner code, the VM creator code, and more, all worked for you, and all are readily and transparently available as FLOSS just a `git clone` away.

**[Syncthing\-Lite](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.github.catfriend1.syncthinglite)**, _Browse Syncthing shares_, is a revival of the old Lite version that was no longer developed. This is the simpler app for quick shares, it does not have the powerful sync capabilities of the full app. 

**[Taler Cashier](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.taler.cashier)** was updated to `1.0.1`, **[Taler Point\-of\-Sale Terminal](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.taler.merchantpos)** to `1.0.3` and **[Taler Wallet](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.taler.wallet.fdroid)** to `1.0.8`. Back in May, we missed the special milestone release of `1.0`, as the software world appears to hold only a symbolic meaning to these numbers. The Taler team wrote about some big news for their ecosystem of apps, and about the future [here](https://www.taler.net/en/news/2025-01.html).

**[Shattered Pixel Dungeon](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.shatteredpixel.shatteredpixeldungeon)** was updated to `3.2.0` with lots of improvements for thrown weapon, buffs, nerfs and more but also with some sad news for users still on Android 4 or older. You can read about these in the developer [July 25 post](https://shatteredpixel.com/blog/coming-soon-to-shattered-thrown-weapon-sets.html) and [August 4 post](https://shatteredpixel.com/blog/shattered-pixel-dungeon-v320.html).

**[WebLibre: Private Browser](https://f-droid.org/packages/eu.weblibre.gecko)**, _Browse privately, search locally, organize tabs, block tracking and use Tor_, is another Firefox fork? _Yes and no_. A browser written in Flutter _(imagine that!)_ that uses [Mozilla Gecko](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Glossary/Gecko), the engine, just like Firefox and our very own Fennec do. It still has a lot to be improved for it to work out smoothly but it is worth a try. Don't forget to offer feedback to the developer. _I know I did._ 😉

**[Wikipedia](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.wikipedia)** updated their app to `r/2.7.50543-r-2025-07-31` but they lost a legal challenge to the new ["Online Safety Act"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_Safety_Act_2023) rules, which it says could threaten the human rights and safety of its volunteer editors. You can read more about this [here](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cjr11qqvvwlo) and hopefully the UK government will listen to the High Court's judgment and protect Wikipedia contributors in the future.

**[Rethink: DNS \+ Firewall \+ VPN](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.celzero.bravedns)** was updated to `v0.5.5o` after one year. Complete rewrite of the network engine 'firestack' for better stability and performance and with ABI builds available, package size reduced. Yet maybe you could postpone updating? The developer saw some issues, like [this one](https://github.com/celzero/rethink-app/issues/2062) and asked us to disable this version. It will take a future cycle to remove this version and update to the next one.

German tech blogger Kuketz had a recent guide on how to _"Hack on Android: Manage Google services on VPN only"_ that used **Rethink**, but he got hit by the issues too. His new post explains [what's going on](https://www.kuketz-blog.de/rethinkdns-im-umbruch-warum-die-aktuellen-pre-releases-mit-vorsicht-zu-geniessen-sind/) (🇩🇪).
