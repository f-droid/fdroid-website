### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Friday, 02 Jan 2026, Week 1

#### Community News

**[Arcticons](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.donnnno.arcticons)**, **[Arcticons Black](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.donnnno.arcticons.light)**, **[Arcticons Day & Night](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.donnnno.arcticons.daynight)** and **[Arcticons Material You](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.donnnno.arcticons.you)** were all updated to `14.0.0`. We skipped a version while fighting reproducibility bugs, but now the latest one is here with more than 14000 icons in total.

**[Einstein Launcher](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.eblan.launcher)** was updated to `0.3.3-alpha`. _Which launcher?_ **Eblan** launcher, got renamed, the rest is still the same.

We just added **[Futon](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.landwarderer.futon)**, _Manga reader with online catalogues_, as a Kotatsu fork. Since **Kotatsu** has stopped development, it will be archived soon so maybe test this new app and switch.

**[LabNex for GitLab](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.labnex.app)** was updated to `7.0.0`, adding some useful features and [many fixes](https://github.com/labnex/LabNex/releases/tag/7.0.0). It still misses one of two must haves for us, but do start testing it out for your GitLab developer mobile workflow.

**[SmartScan](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.fpf.smartscan)** was updated to `1.1.9` as the last version we will provide. The app source repo was archived hence we will archive the app too, in about two days.

[CodeDoctor](https://www.linwood.dev/) left us a gift under the server:

**[Butterfly](https://f-droid.org/packages/dev.linwood.butterfly)** and **[Linwood Butterfly Nightly](https://f-droid.org/packages/dev.linwood.butterfly.nightly)** were updated to `2.4.2`:

> 🚀 Performance optimizations  
> 🌍 RTL layout fixes  
> 🎨 UI improvements

Read more [here](https://www.linwood.dev/butterfly/2.4.2/).
