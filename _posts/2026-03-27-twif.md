### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Friday, 27 Mar 2026, Week 13

#### Community News

**[ArcaneChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.delta.lite)** was updated to `2.46.0` and **[Delta Chat](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.b44t.messenger)** to `2.47.0` and their [changelogs](https://deltachat.github.io/deltachat-android/CHANGELOG#delta-chat-android-changelog) almost match. The team is fairy active in the Fediverse and wants to make people realize that while end-to-end encryption for your messages is nice, the metadata surrounding them might reveal a lot more than one wants. Their latest [infographic](https://chaos.social/@delta/116283784592113619) covers one app from BigTech, but you can compare that with your current solution and strive to limit metadata as much as possible.

**[Fennec F\-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.mozilla.fennec_fdroid)** was updated to `149.0.0` and you can read the changelog [here](https://www.firefox.com/en-US/firefox/android/149.0/releasenotes/). Did you update yet? Then you might have seen the strange notification that now pops-up, about UnifiedPush. It appears it's part of some code reorganization that's not yet done. We are tracking this issue [here](https://gitlab.com/relan/fennecbuild/-/work_items/176) and you can read how to hide the notification so it's no longer annoying. _NOTE: While the word "push" has big corporation connotations, that's just the result of how it was abused. UnifiedPush is an open standard protocol, and we've been covering it since its inception. You can read all about that in our older [post](https://f-droid.org/2026/01/08/unifiedpush-5-years.html)._

In more UnifiedPush news, **[FMD](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.nulide.findmydevice)** and **[FMD edge](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.nulide.findmydevice.edge)** were updated to `0.14.2`. If you're using them with your favorite [UnifiedPush distributor](https://f-droid.org/2022/12/18/unifiedpush.html) maybe double-check if they are registered ok, FMD is working towards [updating this code](https://gitlab.com/fmd-foss/fmd-android/-/work_items/394) and some distributors, like **[Conversations](https://f-droid.org/packages/eu.siacs.conversations/)** _(and forks)_, might not work until ready.

Also **[Prism](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.lonecloud.prism)**, _Privacy\-first UnifiedPush distributor with an optional self\-hosted server_, was just included offering an alternative in this diverse ecosystem.

**[Saracroche](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.cbouvat.android.saracroche)** was updated to `2.7.0` and celebrates one year of existence. You can read _(in French or use Fennec offline translation)_ about the [road behind and the one ahead](https://cbouvat.com/blog/1an-saracroche/).

**[SchildiChat Next](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.schildi.android)** was updated to `0.11.0-ex_26_3_3` after a five month hiatus while reproducibility issues were fixed.

**[WebLibre: The Privacy\-Focused Browser](https://f-droid.org/packages/eu.weblibre.gecko)** was updated to `0.10.0` with [a huge changelog](https://github.com/FaFre/WebLibre/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md#0100). If you want to try an alternative to the Fennec UI, but based on the same engine, you now have more reasons.

[@linsui](https://gitlab.com/linsui) counts to potato:

We've just added two new apps in the [Calculator](https://f-droid.org/categories/calculator/) category to a total of 40+2. While we have so many calculator apps, do all of them calculate? Let's try a simple test: `10000000000.1 - 10000000000`. Simple. The answer should be `0.1`, right? _(Don't cheat, don't open some app, wait and read!)_ Yet some apps don't think so. The new added SoulCalc, Schulrechner and Calculator You told me `0.1000003815`. CalcYou told me `0`, the new Calculator M3 said the same but it's fixed now. CuteCalc, Neumorphic Calculator and Mint Calculator told me `0.10000038146972656`. Qalculate! told me `100⋅10^−3`. PariDroid told me `0.099999999999999999999999999979805160823`. And yetCalc crashes on start and the working version also thinks that the answer is `0.10000038146972656`. Glad to see that most calculators in F-Droid know how to calculate, but looks like this is not a very easy task to achieve.
