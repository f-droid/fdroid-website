### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Friday, 26 Dec 2025, Week 52

#### Community News

**[AFWall\+](https://f-droid.org/packages/dev.ukanth.ufirewall)** was updated to `4.0.1`. We missed a minor fixes version last year, hence this update comes after a two year pause in development. This classic app has now Material Design, new rules management, logging, more security, latest Android support, arm64 support [and more](https://github.com/ukanth/afwall/releases). _The AFWall+ proposition is harder and harder to use, as devices are hard to unlock and gain root control. If you don't have root access, and most of you don't, try our other [VPN-based, no-root firewalls](https://search.f-droid.org/?q=firewall)_.

**[FadCam](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.fadcam)** was updated to `3.0.1`, now with annotations, remote control during live streaming, fragmented MP4, custom watermarks, performance improvements _(45-50% less CPU, 28-33% better battery, 30% less memory)_, a cleaner interface, better settings, improved audio, video corruption fixes, fixed memory leaks, better stability [and more](https://github.com/anonfaded/FadCam/releases/tag/v3.0.0).

While reading device reviews we usually look at performance scores to find out if we want to upgrade our old devices. The test apps are _(mostly?)_ proprietary, which make them either unwanted on our devices or harder to get. We've just included **[Finalbenchmark 2 \- CPU Test](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.ivarna.finalbenchmark2)**, _Comprehensive open source CPU benchmark and performance test_, so hopefully we have an easier way to test. _What's next? Ask reviewers to start using it too, easy, right?_ 

**[kitshn \(for Tandoor\)](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.kitshn.android)** was updated to `2.0.0` adding compatibility with Tandoor v2, overhauled design using Material 3 Expressive, a new Social Media Import function to import Instagram and TikTok posts and [much more](https://github.com/aimok04/kitshn/releases/tag/v2.0.0).

**[LaundryNotes](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.eagsoftware.laundrynotes)** was updated to `2.0` but now it has a new key, [as sometimes these things happens](https://f-droid.org/2023/09/03/reproducible-builds-signing-keys-and-binary-repos.html). Did you install it in the past? Make sure you backup, uninstall and reinstall the new one.

**[Li\-Ri](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.liri.liri)** was updated to `3.1.6` adding experimental TV and controller support. _Use the holidays to test?_

**[NWS Weather Alerts Widget](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.justdave.nwsweatheralertswidget)** was updated to `2.2.3` and brings an app almost entirely rewritten in Kotlin, targeting modern Android, Modern Design, updates for the widget, new way to connect to the NWS services [and more](https://github.com/justdave/nwsweatheralertswidget/releases)

**[Shots Studio](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.ansah.shots_studio)** was updated to `1.9.52` as we had to skip several versions until reproducible builds were fixed. A lot has changed in the last 4 months: Material Design was added, better on-boarding, fonts, more models support, Settings UI was revamped, more languages, smoother animations and zoom, easier collections creation and [plenty of fixes and polish](https://gitlab.com/mohdansah10/shots-studio/-/releases).

**[Stocks Widget](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.github.premnirmal.tickerwidget)** was updated to `4.0.035` and it's back with a FLOSS version.
