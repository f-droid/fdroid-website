### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 19 Jun 2025, Week 25

#### Community News

**[ArcaneChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.delta.lite)** was updated to `1.58.5` and looks like the Delta Chat ecosystem [got a surge of users](https://delta.chat/en/2025-06-04-surge-donations) in the last month.

[@Poussinou](https://github.com/Poussinou) crowdfunds hard:

Framasoft, the makers of **[PeerTube](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.framasoft.peertube/)**, just finished a successful fundraiser to make the app better. Read all the details [here](https://joinpeertube.org/news/crowdfunding-app-end).

[Thore Göbel](https://thore.io/) fights the keepers of gates:

The Swiss site Woz covered _"digital economics"_ in an article covering F-Droid, the origins of Free Software and the Fediverse rise. If you read this TWIF the information might be short and old for you, but [the 10 minutes read](https://www.woz.ch/2525/digitale-oekonomie/die-gatekeeper-umgehen/!M44FC5J9CXJP) might make it a great introduction for your less tech-savvy friends. _(Remember that Fennec has offline, private, translations support, if you can't read German)_

[Vishal](https://forum.f-droid.org/u/vdbhb59) looks at auroras:

**[Aurora Store](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.aurora.store)** was updated to `4.7.2` and fixes a number of crashes and bugs. It also now implements the newer version of API to search and install apps. Notable changes:

* Switch to new APIs for searching and installing apps
* Search filter is no longer available, will be added back later
* Fix crash on navigating to app details page
* Fix crash on spoofing locale
* Translation updates

We also got feedback that the app works better on [Waydroid](https://waydro.id/), so that might be great news for your non-Android devices too.
