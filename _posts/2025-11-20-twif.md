### This Week in F-Droid

#### Community News

**[MBCompass](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.mubarak.mbcompass)** was updated to `1.1.11` after we missed three versions. _What's new?_ Many new [UI improvements](https://github.com/CompassMB/MBCompass/releases/tag/v1.1.9) and [fixes](https://github.com/CompassMB/MBCompass/releases/tag/v1.1.11).

[@CodeDoctor](https://www.linwood.dev) brings us a point one:

> Highlights:
> 
> 💾 Delayed autosave  
> 🖼️ Thumbnail capture improvements  
> 🔁 Template import and export improvements  
> 📄 Improved PDF handling  
> ✍️ Drawing improvements  
> 🗂️ File view and UI improvements  
> 🖱️ Multi-input and tools improvements  
> 🛠️ Stability and performance for large files  
> 
> Read more [here](https://www.linwood.dev/butterfly/2.4.1)

[@linsui](https://github.com/linsui) seeks modernization:

**[ETH & UZH mensa Zürich](https://f-droid.org/packages/ch.famoser.mensa)** was updated to `2.0.4` with a modernized rewrite from a new maintainer.

And hearing echoes from the past, **[Voicesmith](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.jurihock.voicesmith)** was updated to `3.0` after eleven years, featuring a new processing engine and audio API.
