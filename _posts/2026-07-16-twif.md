#### Community News

[@23rd](https://gitlab.com/23rd):

**[Scrib](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.scrib.transcriber)** is a small new app for on-device voice transcription: it turns audio into text entirely on the phone using open Whisper models (whisper.cpp), and only goes online to download a model.

Besides working on its own, Scrib can act as an offline transcription engine for other apps through a small, vendor-neutral AIDL contract (org.opentranscribe.api) — the same idea Offline Translator brought to translation, now for speech-to-text. An app discovers whichever compatible transcriber the user has installed and gets streaming text back, so it never bundles its own model.

[@Licaon_Kter](https://gitlab.com/licaon-kter) converses:

The registrations for [Quicksy](https://f-droid.org/packages/im.quicksy.client/) are permanently closed, as [noted](https://gultsch.social/@daniel/116912137403650656) by its developer [@daniel](https://gultsch.social/@daniel). The reason - "fighting industrial-scale SMS pumping fraud has become unsustainable". Though Daniel is ["committed to keeping the quicksy.im XMPP server running for at least 24 months"](https://gultsch.social/@daniel/116917120533831539), Quicksy users are advised to eventually migrate to [Conversations](https://f-droid.org/packages/eu.siacs.conversations/) by backing up the account in Quicksy and importing it into Conversations (write down the Quicksy password beforehand).

The Kords developer lost their signing key. It is now published with a new appid. The old one has been archived. Please check **[Kords](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.vipedev.kords2)**.
