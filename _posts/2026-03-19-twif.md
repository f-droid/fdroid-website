### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 19 Mar 2026, Week 12

#### F-Droid core

Even if not daring enough to install our latest client in development, we reiterate our message: get people to install F-Droid now, before [it's too late](https://keepandroidopen.org/). It's a small gesture that will prove to be critical for the future of Android as you and me have grown to love!

Now, **[F\-Droid Basic](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fdroid.basic)** was updated to `2.0-alpha5`:

> * Updated strings and translations _(Is your locale 100% [translated](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/f-droid/f-droid/) yet?)_
> * Improved loading of app details page
> * Refresh app lists on app status changes
> * Don't close screenshot when rotating device
> * Fix RTL language presentation of updates
> * ...and more

For CJK users, meaning Chinese, Japanese, and Korean writing systems, we recently fixed a bug in search, if `alpha5` fails to find some apps, this will be fixed in the next version.


#### Community News

["Touchy slide keyboard app will soon require an US corporate account - data to be moved to BigCloud"](https://www.windowscentral.com/software-apps/swiftkey-will-soon-require-a-microsoft-account-data-to-be-moved-to-onedrive) article sounds like some _statement dreamed up by the utterly Deranged_. One year has passed since our own ["Even my keyboard is built reproducibly"](https://f-droid.org/2025/03/04/even-my-keyboard-is-built-reproducibly.html) article was published, and we've been adding and updating keyboard apps on and on, so just [pick one](https://f-droid.org/categories/keyboard-ime/) and use it!!!

**[aTalk](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.atalk.android)** was updated to `5.2.1`, a hot-fix to the big `5.2.0` update. _What's hot?_ [A lot](https://github.com/cmeng-git/atalk-android/blob/5.2.1/aTalk/ReleaseNotes.txt), and the big news is support for the latest [OMEMO](https://conversations.im/omemo/) specification `v0.9` aka _OMEMO 2_. This is great news, but even if OMEMO is well supported in the XMPP ecosystem, [most apps](https://omemo.top) support `v0.3` and none did OMEMO2 on Android before aTalk. We hope this encourages other projects _(ahem and their forks ahem)_ to move forward.

In more messaging news, **[Briar](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.briarproject.briar.android)** was updated to `1.5.17`, keeping Tor up to date. Unfortunately the app appears to be [in maintenance mode](https://code.briarproject.org/briar/briar/-/wikis/changelog), as after the Material Design 3 upgrade it only got minimal development, and that was almost 2 years ago.

**[CastLab](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.fedilab.castlab)** was updated to `1.1.0` last week and it's worth a highlight. Besides bug fixes, it adds some new features: support for the open [FCast](https://fcast.org/) protocol, external display casting via USB-C and Screen sharing.

**[K\-9 Mail](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.fsck.k9)** and **[Thunderbird: Free Your Inbox](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.thunderbird.android)** were updated to `17.0` adding per account avatars _(JPG only for now)_, foldable devices support, printing emails [and more](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-android/releases/tag/THUNDERBIRD_17_0). While **[Thunderbird Beta for Testers](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.thunderbird.android.beta)** was updated to `18.0b2` adding PNG support for avatars and [many fixes](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-android/releases/tag/THUNDERBIRD_18_0b1).

**[LabCoat](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.commit451.gitlab)** has a minor update to `2.8.0` with an authentication token fix. If **[LabNex](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.labnex.app/)** is still not ready for your Gitlab workflow, you can cover some tasks again on the go.

**[Session](https://f-droid.org/packages/network.loki.messenger.fdroid/)** developers are asking for your help, PFS and post-quantum cryptography are still in progress, and more work needs [to be funded](https://getsession.org/blog/a-personal-appeal-from-cofounder-of-session-chris-mccabe).
