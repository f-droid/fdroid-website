### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Friday, 16 Oct 2026, Week 42

#### F-Droid core

Wow, almost a month since our last weekly. A lot has happened, and most stayed the same. We were busy with apps inclusions, apps updates, client fixes and summits. The PR team is also the maintainer team, the testing team and the support team. Priorities take hold.

Now, polishing the Client is a top priority, so we'll talk about apps way below.

**[F\-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fdroid.fdroid)** and **[F\-Droid Basic](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fdroid.basic)** were updated to `2.0.1` and `2.0.2` adding:

> * Show Calculator icon when app hiding is enabled (Full only)
> * Show panic app hiding explanation dialog (Full only)
> * Fix notifications quirks (Thanks proletarius!)
> * Add icon shortcuts for My Apps and Search
> * Sort new apps by added date
> * Fix Huawei/Honor crash when drawing adaptive app icons
> * Fix app installation on old Xiaomi/Redmi devices
> * Disable SessionInstaller on newer Chinese MIUI devices
> * Translation updates

While we had more than a dozen test versions, we still did not convince enough users to update and hit these fixed bugs early in the development process. An understandable problem given the Android ecosystem size and diversity.

**F-Droid Basic** has been suggested for a while, so everyone already got the update. **F-Droid**'s extra features took a bit more time, but we are getting close to a release for everybody. If you've opted into betas you already got the updates, if not, you can do it now or manually expand Versions and update.


#### Community News

XDA covers (their) preferred short list of of must-have FLOSS apps, that made them ["ditch their mainstream rivals"](https://www.xda-developers.com/open-source-android-apps-are-so-good-i-ditched-their-mainstream-rivals/). _Were you even using "mainstream" apps anyway?_

While we acknowledge that mobile computing is the widest available form of computing, by users numbers, we know some also use the bigger "older" kind too, and use them together with their mobile devices to enhance operations. One such tool is [fdroidcl](https://github.com/Hoverth/fdroidcl), a command-line F-Droid desktop client that allows you to get the same apps controls, but from the comfort of your physical keyboard. Matthias Kirschner, of [FSFE](https://fsfe.org/) and former F-Droid Board member, tells us how he uses it for phone setups and upgrades. Get up to speed in this [10 minutes read](https://k7r.eu/the-power-of-fdroidcl/).

**[Conversations](https://f-droid.org/packages/eu.siacs.conversations)** was updated to `2.20.4+free` with minor UI fixes. The developer penned a post that made the rounds in the FLOSS and Android communities, as the app is now also free on _the centralized app store_. _Why now?_ See [the 5 minute read](https://gultsch.de/posts/breaking-up-with-google-play/) that might make you mad that developers are treated this way by faceless corporations and happy that you chose F-Droid.

**[Jami](https://f-droid.org/packages/cx.ring/)** launched their [Jami Web](https://jami.net/jami-web-preview-1/) client, no phone needed.

**[OsmAnd~](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.osmand.plus)** was updated to `5.4.10` with a plethora of fixes polishing the `5.4` release. One important fix that the F-Droid contributors have pushed is OpenGL1 integration crashes as our first release was misbehaving on lower-end devices that can't use the modern OpenGL2 rendered due to hardware constrains.

**[Poker Payout](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.huntercoles.pokerpayout)** was updated to `1.4.22` but with a new signing key. You've installed it last month? Tough luck, you need to uninstall then reinstall.

**[SMSecure](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.jimvixx.smsecure)** was updated to `1.4.0` adding search, better delivery reports, hiding phone numbers in the UI, updated translations and [many more fixes](https://github.com/jimvixx/ShortMessageSecure/releases#release-v1.4.0). One important feature that might get **[Silence](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.smssecure.smssecure/)** users up from the couch has been added, _Import encrypted backups from a SilenceExport folder_. While we kept **Silence** on a life line since its last 2019 version, by fixing `arm64` compatibility, time will soon come for it to be archived as the app won't be installable on future Android versions and the current notification issues are a pain for the users. _Get testing!_
