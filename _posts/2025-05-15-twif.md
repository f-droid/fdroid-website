### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 15 May 2025, Week 20

#### Community News

Looks like not all Nextcloud mobile users are as happy as they can be, and here at F-Droid, we attend to their happiness by building **[Nextcloud](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/com.nextcloud.client/)**, **[Nextcloud Dev](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.nextcloud.android.beta)**, updated to `20250505` today, and [a plethora of related apps](https://search.f-droid.org/?q=nextcloud). But not everyone gets Nextcloud from F-Droid _(for now)_ and they might be frustrated with the app downloaded from third-party centralized stores. The developers have penned a [blog post](https://nextcloud.com/blog/nextcloud-android-file-upload-issue-google/) explaining why the app has limited functionality when installed from there and how they hope future regulation can deal with these kind of issues.

**[Cheogram](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.cheogram.android)** was updated to `2.17.10-1+free`, bringing a fresh initial implementation of Spaces. _What are Spaces?_ Who knows? They have a lot of names, each app/protocol has their own buzzword for this thing: Groups? Communities? Guilds? Clusters? Hubs? Meta-rooms? Pubs? Clubs? Workspaces? Nodes? Sets? Circles? _"A space may contain several rooms, and a room may be contained in several spaces. Spaces can be server spaces used to centralize membership/permissions and make it easier to discover related rooms, they may be client spaces defined by end-users for cross-client grouping of channels in the user interface."_ says the intro to the big ["XMPP Spaces"](https://md.roflcopter.fr/xmpp-spaces#) pad brainstormed back in Nov 2021. _What's next?_ More polish and more clients to adopt and standardize it.

**[Delta Chat](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.b44t.messenger)** was updated to `1.58.3` and its fork **[ArcaneChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.delta.lite)** to `1.58.2`. A new fork-of-the-fork has been included, **[WhatsDown](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.seyfal.whatsdown)**, _⚡ Fast encrypted chats for the family 🎉_, that hopes to improve upon ArcaneChat with new colors, backgrounds, so far, and whatever the future holds.

**[Element X \- Secure Chat & Call](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.element.android.x)** was updated to `25.05.0`. If you see calls misbehaving, a fixed version is already deployed and will be built next.

And in more Matrix client news, **[FluffyChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.fluffy.fluffychat)** was updated to `1.26.1` after 3 months of pause. The changelog is _fluffy_ and full of new stuff, so make sure you [peruse it](https://github.com/krille-chan/fluffychat/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

**[Kahon](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.amanoteam.kahon)**, _A fork of Mihon with some annoying restrictions removed_, was just added. It's a fork-of-a-fork of **Tachiyomi**, which we archived [one year ago](https://f-droid.org/2024/01/25/twif.html).

**[Inure App Manager \(Trial\)](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.simple.inure)** was updated to `build104.1.0` and switched to reproducible builds signed with the upstream developers key. If you had version 103 or older installed, please uninstall it and install the latest version.

The old **Password Store/Password Store (legacy)** app was archived about two years ago and again last year as development stopped (we only have old versions from 4 years ago though since there is no new release since then). This week we included **[Password Store](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.passwordstore.agrahn)**, _Manage your passwords_, a fork that continues the development and keeps with the pace of Android modernization.

[@shuvashish76](https://forum.f-droid.org/u/shuvashish76) listens to the pipes:

We covered the NGI uncertain future back [in July 2024](https://f-droid.org/2024/07/23/ngi-funding-open-letter.html) and this month, Replicant - makers of a free software mobile operating system putting the emphasis on freedom and privacy/security - [tell their story](https://blog.replicant.us/2025/05/future-of-ngi-funding/) about the NGI funding they received and why they signed the letter of support for NGI.

**[NewPipe](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.schabi.newpipe)** was updated to `0.27.7` to fix a YouTube bug where it provided only a 360p stream. The solution employed in this version is likely temporary, and in the long run the [SABR video protocol](https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipe/issues/12248#issuecomment-2850709293) needs to be implemented, but the TeamNewPipe members are currently busy so any help would be greatly appreciated!
