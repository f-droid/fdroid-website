### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Tuesday, 17 Nov 2026, Week 47

#### F-Droid core

F-Droid 2.0 is finally rolling out to all the users! And that's great but not yet perfect. While we had a dozen pre-releases to polish the experience, there are still some devices that move slow to upgrade. If you see the blank screen after the latest update please have patience, it should be done eventually. But, if after 10 minutes it's still there do [reach out](https://f-droid.org/about/#contact) so we can try to debug and conquer this issue too.
