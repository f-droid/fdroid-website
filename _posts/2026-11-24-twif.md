#### Community News

Due to trademark issue, DeepLAndroid was removed and the app comes back as **[DA Translator](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.sakusaku3939.datranslator)**. The users of the old app need to switch to the new one manually since it uses a different application id.

**[KeliBoard](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.gplaider.keliboard)** changes the signing key because the old key is lost. Please reinstall it to get updates.

Many updates failed due to the ONNX Runtime repo being down caused by [a hijacked release](https://www.ox.security/blog/breaking-hijacked-releases-of-microsofts-official-agent-framework-steals-azure-and-ci-credentials/). Builds will come once the repo is back up.
