### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Friday, 05 Jun 2026, Week 23

#### Community News

**[AirPlay Server](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.jqssun.airplay)**, _AirPlay receiver implementation with video and audio support_, was just added pairing with **[Mirror](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.jqssun.displaymirror/)** for streaming between devices or straight from other AirPlay compatible devices out there.

**[Bold Bitcoin Wallet](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.boldwallet)** has a huge update to `4.0.0`, you'll just have to read for yourself [here](https://github.com/BoldBitcoinWallet/BoldWallet/releases/tag/v4.0.0).

**[FairEmail](https://f-droid.org/packages/eu.faircode.email)** was updated to `1.2318` and now it requires Android 6 or later.

**[Inner Breeze](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.naox.inbe)** was updated to `1.5.0` as the last version, the upstream source repo was archived. But, the app has a new start, as we've included **[Inner Breeze](https://f-droid.org/packages/xyz.waozi.inbe)**, yes same name, same developer, but different appid. If you've had the app installed before, better switch now to get updates.

**MakeACopy – OCR Latin \(Best\)** was archived as **[MakeACopy](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.schliweb.makeacopy)**, updated to `4.3.0` this week, already covers its use case. One less app to keep installed.

While the **[Briar](https://search.f-droid.org/?q=briar)** apps are in maintenance mode for a while now, another team has built an app on their protocol. The newly included **[Zerion](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.professor.zerion)**, _Private messaging over Tor\. No phone, no email\. Now with channels\._, promises a lot of [features](https://zerion.chat/index.html#features). Since we've added the latest version you can read the explainer [post](https://zerion.chat/blog/zerion-2-0-release.html) and maybe peek at the [older posts](https://zerion.chat/blog.html) too for more in-depth knowledge.  

[@shuvashish76](https://forum.f-droid.org/u/shuvashish76) feeds our FOMO:

**[App Manager](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.muntashirakon.AppManager/)** celebrates 6 years of existence in a [dev post](https://blog.muntashir.dev/2026/06/01/six-years-of-app-manager/). While we don't track you, the user, we have some downloads stats _(main server only)_ [here](https://grote.gitlab.io/fdroid-metrics-distilled/#app-manager) or [here](https://fdroid.tabler.dev/#:~:text=App%20Manager) or [here](https://fdroid-metrics.streamlit.app/~/+/package_details?package=io.github.muntashirakon.AppManager), that might answer some dev questions.

**[DetoxDroid: Digital Detoxing as Your New Default](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.flx_apps.digitaldetox)** was updated to `2.4.0` helping you doomscroll less. No, you don't need AI or the latest greatest phone released yesterday, DetoxDroid can help you take pauses from the digital realm and keep you in control... of you.

**[Mullvad](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.mullvad.mullvadvpn/)** had a [new security assessment](https://mullvad.net/en/blog/2026-security-assessment-of-our-android-app) of the app with minimal fixes needed.

**[Prav](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.prav.client/)** developers explain how the service is funded and make [an appeal for donations](https://prav.app/blog/funding-our-sms-expenses/) to keep the sign-in function alive. If you believe in decentralized, open protocols for communication, built on FLOSS, **Prav** and its upstream **[Quicksy](https://f-droid.org/packages/im.quicksy.client/)**, are the entry points for your less tech-savvy friends, and they need your help.

**[Torrent Search](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.prajwalch.torrentsearch)** was updated to `0.5.0` with many changes like, torrent details screen, better browser, view markers, better filtering, categories and [more](https://github.com/prajwalch/TorrentSearch/releases/tag/v0.5.0).

**[wallabag](https://f-droid.org/packages/fr.gaulupeau.apps.InThePoche)** was updated to `2.6.0` after a two year pause, with translations updates, fixes and [more](https://github.com/wallabag/android-app/releases/tag/2.6.0).
