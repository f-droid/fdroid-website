### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 13 Nov 2025, Week 46

#### F-Droid core

Without much fanfare, remember the [old CPU issues](https://f-droid.org/2025/08/14/twif.html#f-droid-core) we had? Well... for the last 3 or 4 cycles we have been building apps on our new modern hardware. So stay tuned for more info, more updated apps and faster updates notifications.

The first batch of missed updates starts today...


#### Community News

Bartek of _Wolność w Kieszeni_ or better said of _Freedom in the Pocket_ has written a detailed post (in Polish) about nice apps from F-Droid, entitled ["Unlock your smartphone: Applications"](https://wolnoscwkieszeni.pl/uwolnic-smartfona-aplikacje/). After reading this you can peruse the other posts too as they seem nicely aligned with FLOSS and F-Droid goals.

**[ArcaneChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.delta.lite)** and **[Delta Chat](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.b44t.messenger)** were updated to `2.25.0` improving onboarding, adding more metadata protection, reducing app size and [much more](https://deltachat.github.io/deltachat-android/CHANGELOG#v2250).

**[Kotatsu](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.koitharu.kotatsu)** was updated to `9.4.1` but development has stopped. The developer wrote: _"In light of recent challenges — including threatening actions from Kakao Entertainment Corp and upcoming Google’s [new sideloading policy](https://f-droid.org/2025/10/28/sideloading.html) — we’ve made the difficult decision to shut down Kotatsu and end its support. We’re deeply grateful to everyone who contributed and to the amazing community that grew around this project."_

**[Luanti (former Minetest)](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.minetest.minetest/)** is a popular game, and they've just joined [Open Collective Europe](https://opencollective.com/europe), a non-profit based in Belgium that provides fiscal hosting to open source projects. OCE hosts many notable projects, including EndeavourOS, [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/donate/), and postmarketOS. This allows Luanti to operate like a non-profit and unlocks many new opportunities. Read more [here](https://blog.luanti.org/2025/11/05/non-profit/) to find out how you can help too.

**[Notesnook \- Private notes app](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.streetwriters.notesnook)** was updated to `3.3.8` after a long delay. _What did we miss?_ A new sidebar UI and a tree view for notebooks [at least](https://blog.notesnook.com/notesnook-v3.1.0/), plus a new search, archive and importer [secondly](https://blog.notesnook.com/notesnook-v3.2.0/), and search filters and stats [recently](https://blog.notesnook.com/notesnook-v3.3.0/). Of note, latest version brings [new pricing plans](https://blog.notesnook.com/introducing-new-pricing-plans/) that you might want to read about.

**[OsmAnd~](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.osmand.plus)** had another big update, to `5.2.9`, adding numerous new features and bug fixes. The list is huge so just head over to the dedicated [announcement](https://osmand.net/blog/osmand-android-5-2-released/). _Note: As usual, this update is not yet suggested as stable, if you want to have it now, just enable "Allow beta updates" from the app details menu._

**[Session F\-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/network.loki.messenger.fdroid)** was updated to `1.29.0` with plenty of changes you can read about [here](https://github.com/session-foundation/session-android/releases/tag/1.29.0). Bonus post, the devs talk about "screenshot alerts". _What are those?_ Most decentralized chat users and devs would call them "security theater" so here's a [5 minutes explainer](https://getsession.org/blog/removing-screenshot-alerts).

[@paulali](https://gitlab.com/pmmayero) visited a conference:

> This week there was [Droidcon Kampala](https://www.uganda.droidcon.com/) happening on the 10th to the 11th of November. I had the opportunity to talk about F-Droid and how users can distribute their own apps using F-Droid + Repomaker. The talk was aimed at college students interested in Android development.
> 
> Many thanks to the Droidcon Uganda team and the F-Droid team for making an amazing project.

The slides are available [here](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1KdXEjteSf2VnTz3aG71AYpSdJIHpeu05L9QR4QaY8YU/edit?usp=drivesdk).
