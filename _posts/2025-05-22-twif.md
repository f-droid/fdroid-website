### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 22 May 2025, Week 21

#### Community News

**[Delta Chat](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.b44t.messenger)** and **[ArcaneChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.delta.lite)** were updated to `1.58.4`. If you are a fan of Delta Chat, Chatmail, webxdc and the related projects, maybe you can drop-by in Freiburg, in the Black Forest, between June 7-17th. The [“Decentralized Internet Federation Fusion”](https://delta.chat/en/2025-05-12-diff-invitation) will be an international gathering of the minds and the fans. While you wait for the calendar to switch to June, do try the new Delta Chat Desktop too, [now running on Firefox](https://delta.chat/en/2025-05-22-browser-edition).

**[Jami](https://f-droid.org/packages/cx.ring)** was updated to `20250513-01`, [small improvements](https://jami.net/dev-update-13/) making the app better.

**[Session](https://f-droid.org/packages/network.loki.messenger.fdroid)** was updated to `1.23.1` and the whole network migrated, the other day. The team wrote a [post](https://getsession.org/blog/migrating-from-the-oxen-network-to-session-network) that explains the why and the how.

**Telegram FOSS**, _Telegram is a messaging app with a focus on speed and security_, was archived as development stopped. We recommend you switch to the other still maintained forks like [Forkgram](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.forkgram.messenger/). 

[@linsui](https://gitlab.com/linsui) patches the news:

**[APatch](https://f-droid.org/packages/me.bmax.apatch)** version `11039` was rebuilt, to fix an issue, but looks like we've hit another one. Please avoid this update as the app will never start.

**[Xed\-Editor](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.rk.xededitor)** was updated to `3.1.0`, and Git integration was moved to a standalone extension. The developer also warns users to only download the app from the official sources: F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid and the project Github, as [somebody has put the app on Play without their approval](https://github.com/Xed-Editor/Xed-Editor/releases/tag/v3.1.0). The same person/company has [done the same for WhatSave](https://github.com/mardous/WhatSave/issues/20) unfortunately.
