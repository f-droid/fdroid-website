### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Friday, 29 May 2026, Week 22

#### Community News

**[ArcaneChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.delta.lite)** was updated to `2.50.0` with many fixes and improvements. Calls are no longer experimental, same for channels, location streaming is better and [more](https://github.com/ArcaneChat/android/blob/main/CHANGELOG-upstream.md#v2500).

**[FluffyChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.fluffy.fluffychat)** was updated to `2.6.0` with updated translations, design fixes, sync optimisations, chat filters, room tags, thumbnail control, push notifications for multi account and [more](https://github.com/krille-chan/fluffychat/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md#v260).

**[Jami](https://f-droid.org/packages/cx.ring)** was updated to `20260522-01` and gets _well rounded_, both UI wise and in functionality. We missed the new look back in April, but you can read the developer post [here](https://jami.net/euclid/).

**[NewPipe](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.schabi.newpipe)** was updated to `0.28.7` fixing some annoyances, but also fixing a bit of tech debt by dropping Android 5 support. You can read why [here](https://newpipe.net/blog/pinned/announcement/drop-android-5/), and maybe look up how to either get a compatible newer Android distribution for your device or, as finances permit, acquire a newer device that at least allows a newer one. _The LineageOS Wiki might be helpful in guiding you._ There's also [some news](https://newpipe-ev.de/blog/2026-03-29-two-new-contractors/) regarding team members.

Until then, if you're stuck on 5, **[SkyTube Legacy](https://f-droid.org/packages/free.rm.skytube.legacy.oss)** was updated to `2.999` and might serve you for a bit more.

**[ReFra](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.dot.gallery)** was updated to `4.3.0`. _What is ReFra?_ It's the new name of the **Gallery** app _(yes, rather generic sounding name)_. The changelog is huge, in part because the new features took some time to stabilize and clean up to be fully FLOSS. We have maps integration, yes INTERNET permission is now used, ML (optional) features, app sandboxing processing, 360°, vault overhaul, markup, album groups, FCast, private folder, timeline, subtitles, new video controls, and more. You can read all about these in [the repo](https://github.com/IacobIonut01/ReFra/releases), start on page 2 with `4.1.0` and go up to `4.3.0`.

**[Save by OpenArchive](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.opendasharchive.openarchive.release)** was updated to `4.0.4` adding proofmode for WebDav servers and more bug fixes. The app was designed to be helpful to documenters, archivists, journalists, and advocates around the world, and to face very complex technological and human situations. The developers recently posted about the encountered challenges [here](https://blog.torproject.org/preserving-evidence-openarchive-fosters-accountability-media-sovereignty/).

**[VCMI](https://f-droid.org/packages/is.xyz.vcmi)** was updated to `1.7.4` after a two year hiatus. While the changelog is [huge](https://github.com/vcmi/vcmi/blob/HEAD/ChangeLog.md) and concerns mostly the game itself, the Android part got a new settings screen and QoL improvements for touch screens. If on update it does not start and complains about missing game files, try to reimport the game data files.

**[Offline Translator](https://f-droid.org/packages/dev.davidv.translator)** was updated to `0.6.1` adding a highly wanted feature: _"live camera translation"_. You can see this in action [here](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bf774a4f-3d58-49b7-b4e2-cb30eb395b39).

Some users of older Android versions have seen [crashes](https://github.com/DavidVentura/offline-translator/issues/188) with `0.6.1`, the developer fixed those and we are waiting for the next release to be ready.
