#### F-Droid core

Since F-Droid is an Android "package manager", we advise users to _"use the F-Droid client to get timely updates and info on new apps"_ as, foremost, we want to provide the best experience on the device itself. The website is _for convenience_ only and as the obvious entry point to install the mentioned client. As such, our focus was much more on having a good feature-full client _after_ the user used the website.

We've developed our website at a slower pace than our other projects, first of all because it was already working relatively fine, limiting our improvements only to stream line it _(remember the sidebar issues?)_ or applying fixes, but also because we didn't have contributors specialized in Ruby and Jekyll to know all their gotchas. In the last year several F-Droid fans stepped up and helped close some of our older pain points. Recently [@ray2c](https://gitlab.com/ray2c) fixed our [404 error pages not respecting selected locale](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroid-website/-/issues/834) and fixed Fediverse [post previews mixups](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroid-website/-/issues/794). Reproducible builds got a bit of love [back in May](https://f-droid.org/2025/05/01/twif.html) but users wanted for a long time to see at a glance if the packages are [signed by the upstream developer or by F-Droid](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroid-website/-/issues/289), and now they can.

The website generation uses the older `index-v1` repository format and has the advantage of extra processing power and time to create the pages, while the client switched to the newer `index-v2` format [for the last 3 years](https://f-droid.org/2023/03/01/new-repo-format-faster-smaller-updates.html) and needs to run on device as fast and as slim as possible. There's work in progress to update the website to the newer format and also bring this information to the client in your hands.


#### Community News

On November 15, Dr. Lucas Lasota of FSFE, presented a talk about the impact of the EU Digital Market Act on Free Software. So get a cold/hot beverage, set aside 40+ mins and click [here](https://media.fsfe.org/w/6dbdc15c-e819-41ac-a359-b350fb9582c3).

**[Dib2Qm](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.sourceforge.dibdib.android.dib2qm)** was updated to `0.24.30` but the app is depreciated and the developer advises users to jump to other maintained solutions [like DeltaChat or Simplex](https://f-droid.org/categories/messaging/).

**[Jami](https://f-droid.org/packages/cx.ring)** was updated to `20251126-01` and the developers wrote a post about what free software means, how it started and how it is necessary today and in the future. Spend 5 minutes reading [here](https://jami.net/jami-and-the-gnu-linux-spirit/).

**[NWS Weather Alerts Widget](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.justdave.nwsweatheralertswidget)** was updated to `1.2.1-fdroid1` to add a warning about the depreciated API. The developer is working on [a fix](https://github.com/justdave/nwsweatheralertswidget/issues/61#issuecomment-3604237307). In the mean time, feel free to peruse our [Weather](https://f-droid.org/categories/weather) category.

**[Session F\-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/network.loki.messenger.fdroid)** was updated to `1.30.1`. The team has announced that they are already working on the future `Session Protocol V2`. _What is that?_ It's the next version of the underlying communication protocol of the app that promises to fix some of the missing bits the community asked for: Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS), Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), Linked Devices and more. See the 15 minutes post [here](https://getsession.org/blog/session-protocol-v2). 

**[Stocks Widget](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.github.premnirmal.tickerwidget)** was updated to `4.0.028` as the last FLOSS version. Updates are disabled as we are no longer able to build the app.

**[Unitto — calculator and unit converter](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.sadellie.unitto)** is back after almost two years, updated to version `Titanium yellow`. _What did we miss besides bug fixes?_ AMOLED, Expressive design, updated widgets, and [a lot more](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/releases).
