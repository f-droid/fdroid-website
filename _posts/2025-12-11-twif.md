### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 11 Dec 2025, Week 50

#### F-Droid core

We had great reach with our ["F-Droid and Google's Developer Registration Decree"](https://f-droid.org/2025/09/29/google-developer-registration-decree.html) post and its sequel, ["What We Talk About When We Talk About Sideloading"](https://f-droid.org/2025/10/28/sideloading.html), to raise awareness about the future of Android. But we hope we broke out of our tech-savvy bubble, so everyone hears about what the proposed changes entail, and how F-Droid's goal, of freedom for all users, is truly the way forward.

But if you didn't read those yet, or you want an audio/video to go along, our own board member and writer, Marc Prud'hommeaux, recently had [an 1 hour interview](https://techlore.tv/w/qHetqo141QKU4k3WC3DTp1) that covers it.

While the interview is one month old by now, and Google announced some initial modifications to how this will _presumably_ work, we still believe these are not enough and device owners need full control and freedom.

The [keepandroidopen.org](https://keepandroidopen.org) site has all the info, a call to action and resources so you can make your voice heard.


#### Community News

Which apps make your top trio? In this 5 minutes [post](https://cryptrz.org/wordpress/2025/12/04/3-apps-open-source-qui-facilitent-la-vie/), in French, we get a glimpse of one Luxembourg sysadmins preferences.

[39C3: Power Cycles](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2025/infos/startpage.html), the 39th Chaos Communication Congress will take place between 27-30 December in CCH Hamburg. The **[39C3 Schedule](https://f-droid.org/packages/info.metadude.android.congress.schedule)** app was updated to `1.73.0` so make sure to have it ready.

**[ArcaneChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.delta.lite)** was updated to `2.33.0` to add some [fixes and polish](https://github.com/ArcaneChat/android/blob/main/CHANGELOG-upstream.md#v2331). But hidden there in plain site, there's a boring change _"New experimental feature: several addresses per profile"_ which sounds rather bland, what are profiles anyway? Well... this new option allows one to change the relay _(read it as **server**)_ used to send and receive messages. This is a nice feature and you might need to [read the guide](https://support.delta.chat/t/how-to-migrate-your-profile-to-another-relay-version-2-33/4441) to get the gist on how to switch them. **[Delta Chat](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.b44t.messenger/)** will get updated soon too.

**[FOSS Warn](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.nucleus.foss_warn)** was updated to `1.0.1` and some issues were fixed for UnifiedPush integration, hence **[Conversations](https://f-droid.org/packages/eu.siacs.conversations/)** and forks like **[Cheogram](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/com.cheogram.android/)** or **[monocles chat](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.monocles.chat/)** can be used as distributors again.

**[MediLog](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.zell_mbc.medilog)** was updated to `3.5.0` and we also added **[MediLog \(Reproducible build\)](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.zell_mbc.medilog.fdroid)** signed by the developer.

**[Thunderbird Beta for Testers](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.thunderbird.android.beta)** was updated to `16.0b2` and the devs posted their monthly [report](https://blog.thunderbird.net/2025/12/state-of-the-thunder-14-the-2026-mobile-roadmap/) with the bonus 40+ mins video. _Shock news: Material/You is not good enough and will be dropped!_
