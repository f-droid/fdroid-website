### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Friday, 24 Jul 2026, Week 30

#### Community News

**[Fennec F\-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.mozilla.fennec_fdroid)** was updated to `153.0.0` adding tab groups, another annoying instance of the privacy dashboard _(So, Mozilla, your telemetry showed everybody disabling that from the Start page so you've added it to the Tab page because...? Pfft)_, downloads improvements and bug fixes as usual. One noteworthy announced feature was _"Bookmark import: Import your bookmarks from a CSV file."_ which I've run to use right away... to no avail, because I have no idea how to actually use, or where. _(Ping us if you discover it, please!)_

**[Mattermost Beta](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.mattermost.rnbeta)** was updated to `2.42.2` with the usual bug fixes and rather minimal new features _(those come from the server usually)_. Oh, about the server, `2.41.3` was the last version that was compatible with older server versions down to `9.0` or maybe even lower. If you still run that make sure to update to latest [ESR](https://docs.mattermost.com/product-overview/release-policy.html#extended-support-releases) `v10.11.xx`, it will work even on older Linux distros, just make sure you have Postgresql 13+.

**[Organic Maps・Offline Map & GPS](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.organicmaps)** was updated to `2026.07.15-11-FDroid` with a bunch of improvements, public transport, alternate routes, bookmarks colors, more grids, newer map and Wikipedia files and more. For years, the point of disagreement between the F-Droid team and the OM developers was the flagging of their file server as an anti-feature, ["TetheredNet"](https://f-droid.org/docs/Anti-Features/#TetheredNet), and this is fixed now. In the app you can ~~type~~paste `?map-download-server:https://your-server.com/` in the search window to override the Organic Maps map download servers. ~~Type~~Paste `?no-map-download-server` to remove the override. We welcome this change but we'd like the addition of a proper Setting instead of this contrived trick, just like their fork **[CoMaps \- Hike, Bike, Drive Offline with Privacy](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.comaps.fdroid)**, updated to `2026.07.16-10-FDroid` this week, has.

Talking about maps and navigation, **[OSMAnd~](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/net.osmand.plus/)** had a series of posts we've missed about some of their features you might not have yet explored. Get bold and read on [astronomy](https://osmand.net/blog/astronomy/), [3D buildings](https://osmand.net/blog/3d-buildings/), [nautical maps](https://osmand.net/blog/nautical-maps/) and [measuring tools](https://osmand.net/blog/measuring-tools/).

Speaking of Fennec, **[WebLibre: The Privacy\-Focused Browser](https://f-droid.org/packages/eu.weblibre.gecko)** was updated to `0.20.0` with a [huge changelog](https://github.com/FaFre/WebLibre/blob/v0.20.0/CHANGELOG.md#0200). Will not even try to summarize that, just read it while it installs (or updates)!

[@shuvashish76](https://forum.f-droid.org/u/shuvashish76) disconnect their desktop:
