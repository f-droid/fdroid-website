### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Friday, 11 Sep 2026, Week 37

#### Community News

Our fight with categories has been long and fruitful, more were added, soon more will be removed, perfection needs shuffling apps around in the hopes users find it easier to locate good apps that fulfill their needs.

Thanks to [@linsui](https://gitlab.com/linsui) for aggregating the metadata and taking time to hunt appropriate apps. The new categories are:

* [Emergency Action](https://f-droid.org/categories/emergency-action/) - for your device, for your person, for your friends and family
* [Medication](https://f-droid.org/categories/medication/) - you need to take it, let's make it as seamless and as easy as possible
* [Speech Recognizer](https://f-droid.org/categories/speech-recognizer/) - put those fingers to rest
* [Text Encryption](https://f-droid.org/categories/text-encryption/) - in a bottle, with a pigeon or via magic electric packets over the wire, they need to be private

**[CoMaps \- Hike, Bike, Drive Offline with Privacy](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.comaps.fdroid)** was updated to `2026.08.31-14-FDroid` with some nice [changes](https://codeberg.org/comaps/comaps/releases/tag/v2026.08.31-14). One innovative new feature, that FLOSS maps users have been asking for year by now, is the integration of Mangrove user reviews for points of interest. For now they can only be seen, there's work in progress to add edit options too. You can take a sneak peak on how many of these are in your area, using [this page](https://altilunium.github.io/MangroveViewer/) _(zoom it)_. Also TIL: **[MapComplete](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.mapcomplete/)** has a way to view/edit reviews. _The more the merrier!_

**[Joplin](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.cozic.joplin)** was updated to a new major version, `3.7.8`. The [changelog](https://joplinapp.org/news/20260831-release-3-7) looks enticing, so make sure both your server and app are updated in order to enjoy the enhancements. Of note, the server has an integrated [MCP server](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/2026-07-28/getting-started/intro) that allows your notes to be processed by...  _you know what_, if you wish so.

**[LocalSend Kotlin](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.mouse233.localsendkotlin)**, _An unofficial, lightweight Kotlin LocalSend client for older devices_, is now in F-Droid. **[LocalSend](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.localsend.localsend_app/)** is a great app for transfers between many devices, it's written in Flutter and supports Android 7 and later. But at 46Mb it's rather hefty, and maybe you need transfers to/from your older devices too. Here is where the new Kotlin app shines, it's lightweight in size, 10 times smaller _(no joke)_, but still allows fast & secure transfers, and it supports Android 5 and 6 too.

**[Monica F\-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/takagi.ru.monica.fdroid)**, _Open\-source password manager: local encryption, autofill, TOTP, Passkey_, was just added. We have more than 60 apps in the [Password & 2FA](https://f-droid.org/categories/password-2fa/) category, but while **[AIPOS Password Manager](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.aipos.aipospm)**, updated to `1.2.2` this week, and [PassVault](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.jksalcedo.passvault/) could import Bitwarden data, this new app is the first one that can also sync that data _(with self-hosted Vaultwarden instances)_ too.

**[OsmAnd~](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.osmand.plus)** was updated to `5.4.4` with a huge update. Just read the release [post](https://osmand.net/blog/osmand-android-5-4-released/) to get the full view on what's new. My favorite fix: _"Fixed navigation incorrectly ending after switching to another app and returning to OsmAnd"_, wow, that was really annoying. For our Magyar users, it appears that your map issue has been fixed, do test! _NOTE: The cleaning procedure for **OSMAnd~** publishing is extensive, we don't immediately push new updates to everyone, so brave users get a chance to drive it around and report any issues. If you want to install it now, you can go to the app page in F-Droid client, expand Versions and manually update or enable "Allow beta" in the upper right menu._

We know that **OSMAnd~** while being feature full it's also very heavy resource wise, we have more OpenStreetMap based maps apps, like **CoMaps** above and **[Organic Maps・Offline Map & GPS](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.organicmaps)**, updated to `2026.08.27-18-FDroid` this week, and you might find them better for your use case/device.

**[SimpleX Chat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.simplex.app)** was updated to `7.0.2` and missed last week was the announcement of the start of their crowdfunding campaign. Do read how to [get a stake in SimpleX](https://simplex.chat/blog/20260819-simplex-chat-crowdfunding.html) and a testimonial from an [early investor](https://simplex.chat/blog/20260908-nick-rogers-why-i-backed-simplex-chat.html). Also, don't forget to bookmark the September 15 [livestream event](https://simplex.chat/livestream/) and the December 12 [domains countdown](https://simplex.domains/).

**[Sky Map](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.google.android.stardroid)** was updated to `2.0.3:Apollo` with a full rewrite. Many things have [changed](https://github.com/sky-map-team/stardroid/blob/v2.0.3/stardroid-v2/README.md#whats-new-in-v2) and this opens the path for better development and nicer features. 

If **Sky Map** seems too simple for your view of the Universe, **[Celestia](https://f-droid.org/packages/space.celestia.mobilecelestia)** was updated to `26.8.1` and brings you the full 3D experience of Space.

**[Taler Wallet](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.taler.wallet.fdroid)** was updated to `1.6.7` with important security fixes, so make sure you update soon.

**[Tsacdop \- Podcast Player](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.stonegate.tsacdop)** was updated to `1.0.2` after a five year pause, _welcome back!_

[@shuvashish76](https://forum.f-droid.org/u/shuvashish76) digs deep into the kernel:

**[AnkiDroid: Flashcards](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.ichi2.anki)** was updated to `2.24.1` fixing some security issues [and more](https://ankidroid.org/docs/changelog.html#_version_2_24_1_20260831), do update as soon as possible. If your friends use some other centralized store, make sure they update too, or if they can't _(update not available, or... app gone)_ then do guide them to get the app from F-Droid.

**[App Manager](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.muntashirakon.AppManager)** was updated to `4.1.1`, adding an interesting feature "Revoke Internet Permission via [eBPF](https://ebpf.io/what-is-ebpf/)". You need to read [the news](https://github.com/MuntashirAkon/AppManager/releases/tag/v4.1.1) about how that works and its limitations. Our [Firewall](https://f-droid.org/categories/firewall/) category has more than twenty apps, yet not everyone can use their local VPN method, so any new tricks might help. As usual, keep in mind that apps might use other ways to get access to or info from the Internet, like using other apps _(Google Services, UnifiedPush provider, etc)_ so don't be surprised when this happens.

If we talk about centralized stores, **[Fedilab](https://f-droid.org/packages/fr.gouv.etalab.mastodon)** was updated to `3.43.3` with [fixes](https://codeberg.org/tom79/Fedilab/releases/tag/3.43.3). What's more noteworthy is that F-Droid is now the only place for users to get the app, as _the other store_ asked the dev to [censorship competing services](https://toot.fedilab.app/@apps/117220424041848200) and more awful things. The backup and restore process to switch to F-Droid is easy, so get started and announce your Fedi friends too! _Notifications worry you? We have plenty of [UnifiedPush apps](https://f-droid.org/categories/push/) that will take care of that._
