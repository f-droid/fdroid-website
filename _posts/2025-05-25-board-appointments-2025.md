Dear members of the F-Droid community,

We received a number of submissions after [opening nominations](https://f-droid.org/2025/03/30/board-of-directors-nominations.html) from the community.
Thank you to everyone who made a nomination and everyone who volunteered for their interest in serving on the Board of Directors!

I am delighted to announce our new appointments to the F-Droid Board of Directors for 2025.
Please give a warm welcome to Vishal Bakhai, Marc Prud'hommeaux and Neil Brown!

Vishal, who goes by the handle vdbhb59 on the [F-Droid Forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/), will be familiar to many of you through his extensive participation in the F-Droid community as a moderator:

> “F-Droid is a logical place to understand and amicably connect to the FOSS/FLOSS community.
> Being an avid OSS & F-Droid user myself, I would love to be on board where we all aim towards a common goal, and bring freedom of choice to the community at large.
> I also aim to assist with my moderation roles while gaining knowledge and network of far-reaching stakeholders in return.”
> \- Vishal Bakhai

Marc joins us with a wide range of experience, including as founder of the [App Fair Project](https://appfair.org/), as well as deep technical knowledge of software development for mobile devices:

> “In these fraught times - both technologically and politically - projects like F-Droid serve as an essential touchstone of individual digital sovereignty.
> The ability to create and run software free of gatekeeper control has never been more in jeopardy than it is right now.
> As a software developer with decades of experience, and as someone who has been shipping mobile apps both independently and through the commercial app stores for 15 years, I am delighted at the opportunity to use my expertise to help guide and grow the F-Droid project in the coming months and years.”
> \- Marc Prud'hommeaux

Neil is a true veteran of the FOSS (Free and Open Source Software) community, and brings legal expertise which will be essential for securing the ability of F-Droid to serve users in an increasingly complex regulatory environment:

> “I'm an Internet and computer lawyer, with a long history of giving pragmatic, grounded advice on thorny legal and policy issues, and an even longer history of using and supporting Free software.
> With the ever-growing number, scope, and reach of laws and regulations around the world applying to software and online services, I'm keen to support F-Droid in providing a world-class, easy-to-use, ethical alternative to proprietary software distribution systems.”
> \- Neil Brown

We are bidding farewell to four individuals who have been instrumental in the governance of F-Droid as [founding members](https://f-droid.org/2023/03/20/f-droid-board.html) of the Board of Directors: Michael Downey, Matthias Kirschner, Morgan Lemmer-Webber and Andrew Lewman.
We would like to thank them for their service to the F-Droid community. 

The F-Droid Board of Directors has a number of designated roles, which we have reassigned for this year.
Peter will be Clerk, with Neil as Vice Clerk.
Vishal will be Treasurer.
John and I will continue as Vice Chair and Chair respectively.

The composition of the Board of Directors is now as follows:

- Vishal Bakhai (Treasurer) - until 2027
- Neil Brown (Vice Clerk) - until 2027
- Sebastian Crane (Chair) - until 2026
- Marc Prud'hommeaux - until 2027
- Peter Serwylo (Clerk) - until 2026
- Juliana Sims - until 2026
- Hans-Christoph Steiner - *ex officio*, until 2026
- John Sullivan (Vice Chair) - until 2026

*Please note: I wrote this announcement in May 2025, but it was not published at that time; it was published retroactively in April 2026. I apologize for any confusion caused by the late publication!*
