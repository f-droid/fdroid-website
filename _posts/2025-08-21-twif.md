### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 21 Aug 2025, Week 34

#### Community News

The Matrix ecosystem fixed some major bugs that affected servers and clients too. The CVE level issues were announced back [in July](https://matrix.org/blog/2025/07/security-predisclosure/) but things were intentionally kept vague while developers worked hard on yet another room version bump and fixes.

Last week the [big post](https://matrix.org/blog/2025/08/project-hydra-improving-state-res/) dropped, explaining the nitty-gritty about what happened, what was fixed, how and what to expect.

F-Droid has several clients that got updated with these fixes:

* **[Element \- Secure Messenger](https://f-droid.org/packages/im.vector.app)** was updated to `1.6.44`
* **[SchildiChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.spiritcroc.riotx)** was updated to `1.6.44.sc88`
* **[FluffyChat](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.fluffy.fluffychat)** was updated to `2.1.0`

And we recommend that you update as soon as possible. 

But we also host **[Element X](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.element.android.x/)** and its fork **[SchildiChat Next](https://f-droid.org/packages/chat.schildi.android/)** which are not yet updated as these hit reproducibility issues that hamper determinism. _(These issues are not related to any other F-Droid issue from last month and upstream developers are working on fixes.)_

While we don't usually advise this, as the whole purpose of having F-Droid as a third party that verifies the build can't be exerted now, users of **Element X** and **SchildiChat Next** can update directly from the developers as the apps are signed by them already.

**[Ente Photos \- Encrypted photo storage](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.ente.photos.fdroid)** was updated to `1.2.0` adding [custom layouts, an advanced editor, smart albums and performance fixes](https://ente.io/blog/photos-v1.2/).

**[FlorisBoard](https://f-droid.org/packages/dev.patrickgold.florisboard)** was updated to `0.5.0` after five intense months of development. The changelog is hefty and no wonder, given [all the nice changes and fixes](https://github.com/florisboard/florisboard/releases/tag/v0.5.0).

**[Immich](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.alextran.immich)** was updated to `1.138.0` with the new beta timeline, PIN reset, OAuth ID [and much much more](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v1.138.0).

**[Rethink: DNS \+ Firewall \+ VPN](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.celzero.bravedns)** was updated to `v0.5.5q` and this should fix the issue users encountered with the last update. Do test and reach out if still problematic.

**[Session F\-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/network.loki.messenger.fdroid)** was updated to `1.27.0`. The developers have written a post about [common network troubleshooting](https://getsession.org/blog/regional-connectivity) for users having issues connecting.

Full changelog [here](https://github.com/koreader/koreader/releases/tag/v2025.08).

**[LibreTorrent](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.proninyaroslav.libretorrent)** was updated to `4.0.1` with Android 15 support and a nice Material 3 (You) redesign.

**[Red Screen](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.vi_server.red_screen)** was updated to `1.2` after 10 years! If you needed a red screen, now it's the time to install. Spoiler alert: _it has shortcuts for more colors._
