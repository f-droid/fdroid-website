### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 09 Oct 2025, Week 41

#### Community News

[The end of tt-rss.org](https://community.tt-rss.org/t/the-end-of-tt-rss-org/7164) might be a reason to be sad. We have at least two apps that connect to such instances, **[TTRSS-Reader](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.ttrssreader/)** and **[Geekttrss](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.geekorum.ttrss.free/)**, but looks like development continues at [github.com/tt-rss](https://github.com/tt-rss).

[@linsui](https://gitlab.com/linsui) updates to version 2:

**[Immich](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.alextran.immich)** was updated to `2.0.1` marking a 'Stable Release of Immich' with DVDs and shirts as merch to celebrate. Get the [news post](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/discussions/22546), dig into the [2.0.0](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v2.0.0) changelog and the [2.0.1](https://github.com/immich-app/immich/releases/tag/v2.0.1) fix.


#### Removed Apps

##### 2 apps were removed

* Memento, _Take notes_ was discontinued a long time ago, switch to the currently developed, named the same, **[Memento](https://f-droid.org/packages/de.mwarning.memento/)** fork.
* RiMusic, _A multilingual Android application for streaming music from YouTube Music_, had a drama filled end, development stopped, source archived. **[Kreate](https://f-droid.org/packages/me.knighthat.kreate)**, a fork of it, was updated to `1.8.1-fdroid` this week, so maybe try to switch.

#### Newly Added Apps

##### 1 app was newly added

* **[Pokéverse](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.aditya1875.pokeverse)**: Explore, collect, and manage your Pokémon universe in one app\!

#### Downgraded Apps

##### 1 app was downgraded

* **[Nextcloud Notes](https://f-droid.org/packages/it.niedermann.owncloud.notes)** was downgraded from `4.5.0` to `4.5.0 RC1` as it was crashing.
