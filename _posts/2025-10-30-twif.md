### This Week in F-Droid

### TWIF curated on Thursday, 30 Oct 2025, Week 44

#### Community News

**[Fossify Messages](https://f-droid.org/packages/org.fossify.messages/)** update `1.5.0` from last week has been having some crashing issues with heavy conversations, tracked by the developer [here](https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Messages/issues/574). If you've encountered them, you are advised to uninstall, install older version `1.4.0` and use the upper right menu in client to "Ignore this update" until the next one _(built while you read this)_ comes.

**[PrivacyFlip](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.dorumrr.privacyflip)** was updated to `1.4.1` and features a key change unfortunately. Things happened with the old key, [as they do](https://f-droid.org/2023/09/03/reproducible-builds-signing-keys-and-binary-repos.html), and a new one has been born. If you've installed the app in the past we recommend you uninstall and install the newly signed one today.

**[SwatchIt](https://f-droid.org/packages/net.damschen.swatchit)**, _Manage your knitted swatches_, was just added. _What is it?_ It's a choose your own adventure game that involves quests you solve with yarn and needles, I guess. [Here](https://katharina.damschen.net/post/2025-09-10-swatchit-on-codeberg/)'s a bit of a background of how the app came to be. And to gain extra _experience_, you can read about the developer's quest to add their app to F-Droid, if you have 15 minutes to spare, [here](https://katharina.damschen.net/post/2025-10-27-publish-to-f-droid/).

[@samanbaia](https://forum.f-droid.org/u/samanbaia/summary) spells the magic words:

**Lineage, F-Droid, XMPP** will summon an one hour long [video presentation](https://acloud2.zaclys.com/index.php/s/MWL6xygARb9wRgM?dir=/&editing=false&openfile=true) and the corresponding [slides](https://acloud2.zaclys.com/index.php/s/JKQe2AFrDGCrm5T?dir=/&editing=false&openfile=true), both in Italian. Bonus QR codes spread around to poll you on your choices. This was part of Linux Day Torino 2025, that took place on October 25th.

[@shuvashish76](https://forum.f-droid.org/u/shuvashish76) has bad and good news:

**[Immich](https://f-droid.org/packages/app.alextran.immich/)** is the [fourth most starred](https://fdroid.tabler.dev/) app in our repo and one with [a healthy following](https://fdroid-metrics.streamlit.app/~/+/package_details?package=app.alextran.immich#search-packages). Earlier this month they ended up having their services inaccessible at Google's whims as all of their `*.immich.cloud` websites were marked as dangerous and users started being shown the dreaded "red-screen-of-death" page. _How did our heroes fight the beast?_ You can read it all in the 5 minutes long [post](https://immich.app/blog/google-flags-immich-as-dangerous). _One word comes to mind after reading this, can you guess which? (scroll to the end to see if we think alike)_

**[LibreTube](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.github.libretube)** was updated to `0.29.0`. After half a year of work, this is perhaps one of their biggest releases ever _(more than 500 commits)_, including: Material 3 Expressive redesign, edge-to-edge support, predictive back and [much more](https://github.com/libre-tube/LibreTube/releases/tag/v0.29.0).
