F-Droid is powered by your donations! Donate to keep free software thriving.

### Why your donation matters

Received donations are an integral part of F-Droid as they support in:

* Server maintenance and support
* Administration, development and project maintenance
* Travel sponsorship for core contributors to organize, attend and host sessions at conferences, helping create an even bigger impact.
* Outreach materials

### How to donate

We recommend using donation platforms like [**Liberapay**](https://liberapay.com/F-Droid-Data/ "https://liberapay.com/F-Droid-Data/") or [**Open Collective**](https://opencollective.com/f-droid/ "https://opencollective.com/f-droid/"). These free software, community-driven donation platforms, support low-fee and transparent donations.

You can support F-Droid via monthly recurring donations or with one-time gifts.

Monthly donations keep F-Droid sustainable. By setting up a recurring monthly donation, you create a dependable funding stream that helps us plan ahead, support development initiatives and maintain essential services without interruptions.

The small gift of €5/month, when multiplied by thousands of supporters, results in a reliable funding stream for maintenance, innovation, security and outreach programs. Donors that support us on a recurring basis are key allies, helping us realize F-Droid's core mission - fighting for software freedom and privacy protection. Your recurring support empowers long term impact that one-time donations can't match.

### Custom Donations

If your donation amount or frequency does not fit into one of the other categories, feel free to donate a custom amount once or multiple times per year. Your support helps sustain the project, its mission of user freedom and open, trustworthy software for everyone.

### Institutional Support and Large Gifts

Major gifts help turn community momentum into durable impact, supporting maintenance, security improvements, and the behind-the-scenes work that keeps the project stable. If your foundation or organization funds open-source sustainability, privacy, or public-interest tech, we are happy to discuss ways your support can make a lasting difference.


### What payment methods can I use?

We only support the payment methods that our fiscal hosts (legally registered organizations that hold and manage funds for F-Droid) offer. The fiscal host determines which payment methods are available as shown below:

Visit the info pages of our fiscal hosts to get more details on their fees:

* [Liberapay](https://liberapay.com/about/faq#fees)
* [Open Collective Europe ASBL](https://opencollective.com/europe)
* [Open Source Collective 501(c)(6)](https://opencollective.com/opensource)
* [GitHub Sponsors](https://docs.github.com/en/billing/managing-billing-for-github-sponsors/about-billing-for-github-sponsors)

## FAQs Donations

#### What payment methods can I use to donate?

Open Collective, Liberapay, and direct bank transfer.

#### Can I donate just once, or do I have to set up recurring donations?

You can donate once, or set up recurring donations. Both are supported.


#### Will I receive updates about how my donation impacts F-Droid?

Yes! You can follow along in our weekly blog posts, in the forum, or in GitLab to track development progress.
