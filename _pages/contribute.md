### Contribute
F-Droid is powered by volunteers from all over the world. Whether you’re a developer, translator, tester, or just someone who loves helping others, there’s a place for you here.

Every contribution, big or small, helps keep F-Droid resilient, diverse, and open to everyone.

### Ways You Can Contribute

**Help the Community** 

**You can help build a welcoming, empowered community by:**

* Answering questions from new users or developers in our [forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/) or [chat]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/#contact)
* Joining the conversation in our [various channels]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/#contact)
* Sharing tips and guides to make F-Droid easier to understand and use
* Following our [Code of Conduct]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/Code_of_Conduct). We want this to be a positive and welcoming space for everyone

### Report & Track Issues

**Take part in making F-Droid stronger and more reliable by:**

* Reporting bugs in our [issue tracker]({{ site.baseurl }}/issues/)
* Helping us manage issues by confirming bugs, adding labels, reviewing merge requests, and asking follow-up questions

### Submit & Update Apps

**Contribute to expanding user choice and app diversity by:**

* Submitting a new app to the main repo, just read the [inclusion policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/Inclusion_Policy) first
* Requesting a new app via the [Requests for Packaging](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/rfp/issues) tracker
* Writing metadata and submitting it through the [F-Droid Data repo](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroiddata/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) to speed up inclusion
* Keeping apps up to date by contributing metadata for new versions.
* Check out additional [documentation]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/) for more info

### Translate & Localize

**Be part of making F-Droid accessible to our global community by:**

* Improving F-Droid's translations through our [translation platform](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/f-droid/)
* Adding missing languages and updating existing ones to keep F-Droid available to everyone
* Joining the [translation forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/c/translation) section to collaborate with others
* Joining the conversation in our [translation channels]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/Translation_and_Localization/#contact)
* Check out additional [documentation]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/Translation_and_Localization/) for more info

### Test Releases

**Ensure quality, security, and trust by:**

* Testing F-Droid versions before they're official.
* Reporting bugs for faster fixes
* Reviewing translation updates on [staging.f-droid.org](https://staging.f-droid.org/)

### Develop

**Power innovation and open technology**

Our code is hosted on GitLab. Main projects include:

* The [Android client app](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroidclient)
* [Server tools](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroidserver) for running repositories and building apps
* [Metadata](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroiddata) for apps in the main repository
* [Website and documentation](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroid-website)

Start by cloning a project and submitting a merge request. Want to see a bigger change? Join our [forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/) or [contact us]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/#contact) to coordinate.

### Maintain Infrastructure

**Keep the ecosystem resilient and sustainable by:**

* Helping manage servers, builders, our web portal, download areas, and deployments
* Providing support for our Ansible-based deployment 
* Reach out via [our channels]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/#contact) or email at [team@f-droid.org](mailto:team@f-droid.org)

### Explore Data & Metrics

F-Droid has a rich dataset of build logs, app metadata, and APK details. Discover and share our impact in numbers by:

* Analysing app ecosystem trends to help us track growth and make predictions
* Exploring privacy/security metrics to help us improve services and communicate impact
* Building tools with our [APIs]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/All_our_APIs/) to add to the existing ecosystem

### Join the Movement!

* Start with our [documentation]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/)
* Join the [forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/) or contact us via [our channels]({{ site.baseurl }}/about/#contact)
* Find a task that matches your skills or just dive in and start asking questions!

F-Droid thrives because people like you care about freedom, privacy, and open source software.
